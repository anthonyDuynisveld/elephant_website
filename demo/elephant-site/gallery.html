Image Gallery

A collection of photographs showcasing the beauty and majesty of African elephants in their natural environment.

images/elephant-savanna.jpg

Elephant on the Savanna

An adult African bush elephant crosses the golden grasslands of the Serengeti at sunset. These animals can travel up to 50 kilometers in a single day while foraging.

images/elephant-water.jpg

Cooling Off at the Waterhole

A family group bathes and drinks at a waterhole in Amboseli National Park. Elephants drink up to 200 liters of water per day and use their trunks to spray water and mud over their skin for cooling.

images/elephant-calf.jpg

Mother and Calf

A mother elephant walks alongside her young calf in Kruger National Park. Calves stay close to their mothers for the first several years of life, learning essential survival skills from the herd.

images/elephant-tusks.jpg

Impressive Tusks

A mature bull elephant displays large ivory tusks. Tusks are elongated incisor teeth that grow throughout the elephant's life. They are used for digging, stripping bark, and defense.

images/elephant-herd.jpg

The Herd

A large family herd moves together across the Masai Mara. Herds are led by the oldest female, known as the matriarch, and typically consist of related females and their young.

images/elephant-dust.jpg

Dust Bath

An elephant throws dust over its back with its trunk. Dust bathing helps protect their sensitive skin from sunburn and parasites, and is a common daily ritual.
